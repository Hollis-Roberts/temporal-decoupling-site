The framework does not claim to explain everything. It claims to explain specific, testable phenomena from a single structural hypothesis. Each prediction includes a quantitative falsification threshold:

| Prediction | Expected Result | Falsification Threshold |
|------------|-----------------|-------------------------|
| Qubit coherence at L2 | $250\times$ improvement | $< 50\times$ with controlled $\Gamma_{\text{env}}$ |
| Atomic clocks near DM | Drifts correlate with lensing maps | No correlation with sufficient sensitivity |
| Void Hubble constant | $H_{\text{void}} \approx 66 \pm 1.5$ | $H_{\text{void}} > 69$ or $< 62$ |
| Dark energy $w(z)$ | $\Delta w(z) \approx 0.015$–$0.025$ at $z \in [0.5, 1.5]$ | $w(z) = -1.00 \pm 0.01$ strictly |
| Intermediate states | Decay $\tau < 10^{-20}$ s | Stable non-resonant particles detected |

A theory that cannot be broken is not science. This one can.
