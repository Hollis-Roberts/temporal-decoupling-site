Before time, before space, before particles—there is a background medium we call the Timeless Sea (TSC). It is not empty. It is a structured, scalar field containing oriented one-dimensional threads called **pre-temporal worldlines**. These threads do not move, do not age, and do not interact—until a timeline boundary sweeps through them. That sweep is the beginning of everything we observe: time, gravity, matter, and light. The TSC is not a theory of "what happened." It is a theory of "what underlies."

<summary>Technical Details</summary>
The TSC is modeled as a static background scalar medium with no intrinsic time parameter. Pre-temporal worldlines are oriented 1D structures defined by a direction vector $\mathbf{u} \in S^2$ and a coherence scale $\ell_{\text{TSC}}$. The timeline hypersurface $\Sigma$ sweeps through the TSC with velocity $v_\Sigma$. Intersection events generate observable phenomena via the chronometric coupling parameter $\Gamma(\rho) \propto \rho^n$, where $n \approx 0.04 \pm 0.01$.
