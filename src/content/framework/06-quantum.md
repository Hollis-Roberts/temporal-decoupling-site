Entangled particles are not separate entities communicating across space. They are two intersection points on the **same flow line**. Measurement forces a crease, revealing entry and exit loci with opposite spins—a geometric mirror reflection, not spooky action. Tunneling is not wavefunction penetration; it is a temporary decoupling cycle: $\Gamma \to 0$ through the barrier, then recoupling beyond it.

<details>
<summary>Technical Details</summary>
Entanglement lifetime scales with $\rho$. Tunneling probability depends on the likelihood that $\Gamma$ drops sufficiently before reflection. Gravitational modulation and temporal asymmetry are testable signatures distinguishing geometric from informational models.
</details>
